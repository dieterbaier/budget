Getting the model to describe the budget the owner actually keeps, and fixing the
words it is described in.

== Where we are

The register exists and is authoritative. Every domain concept has one entry
fixing the term the owner speaks, the identifier, and the display form ADR-023
needs.

ADR-022 rule 3 is no longer staged. The four identifiers implemented before the
register are renamed: `Fixkostenposition`, `Zahlungsintervall`, `letzteZahlung`,
`monatsanteil()`, and in the schema `fixkostenpositionen`, `zahlungsintervall`,
`letzte_zahlung`. The schema moved through a new migration,
`V3__fixkostenposition_terms.sql`, because ADR-010 now says an applied migration
is never edited. The old names remain only in `V1__initial_schema.sql` and in
`V3`, which renames them.

Two things #79 left alone on purpose: `fixedCostsMonthly` belongs to
`MonthlyExpenditure`, which has no register entry and is #69's to name; ADR-009
and ADR-014 still say `FixedCost`, which waits on the decision in #76.

Chapter 8 leads with the domain model diagram rather than a bulleted list, drawn
from ADR-009's aggregate map. `Verwendungszweck` is dashed there because its
aggregate boundary is not decided.

The glossary, chapter 8 and ADR-022 all carry `reviewed: false`: the terms are
the owner's, the prose is not, and his acceptance is what flips them.

== Open in this topic

*Two implemented types name concepts with no register entry at all* —
`TransactionType`, whose `EXPENSE` value has no term, and `MonthlyExpenditure`.
Registering them by hand is the wrong move: #69 is the check that finds this
class systematically, and it should find them rather than be told about them.

*The six ADRs in #76 need a decision before that issue starts* — leave them as
historical record, or annotate them. Several, including ADR-022 itself, use the
retired renderings legitimately as examples of the error they name.

`Verwendungszweck`'s aggregate boundary outlives this topic and is recorded in
`diary/open-threads.adoc`.
