== Day 1 — 24 July 2026: the walking skeleton

Scope was deliberately cut to the bone: a single-user actuals tracker. Not a
budgeting product, not multi-tenant, no auth beyond a single deployment
(ADR-007).

The first slice went end to end rather than layer by layer — domain, ports,
JPA/Flyway adapter, HTTP adapter, React PWA — so that the architecture was
exercised by something real before it was written down.

Decisions recorded on the way: DDD with domain identity (ADR-009), Flyway
(ADR-010), no Lombok (ADR-011), explicit bean configuration (ADR-012).

ADR-009 is the one that kept paying out. Our position — *"I don't like
to have technical IDs as identifiers for my domain objects"* — looked like a
preference on day 1. It settled two much harder arguments on day 4, and neither
of them was about identity on its face.

That is the argument for recording a decision whose consequences are not yet
visible: the value of ADR-009 was not in what it decided but in what it made
*derivable* later. Three days on, "must category names be unique?" and "how can
a name be an identity if names can change?" both had answers already, and
neither needed a new decision.
