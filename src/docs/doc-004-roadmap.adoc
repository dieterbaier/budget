[[roadmap]]
= Roadmap
:id: DOC-004-roadmap

== Increment 0 — Bootstrap Setup

A short setup flow to seed the master data: accounts (current account, reserve
call-money account for non-monthly fixed costs, savings call-money account),
category groups, categories, fixed-cost definitions with amount, interval, and
anchor date, and the current balance of each account. This replaces the manual
Notion setup.

== Increment 1 — Walking Skeleton

Hexagonal Spring Boot backend, PostgreSQL, and an installable React PWA. Record a
transaction from a bank statement (date, amount, category, account, type) and
show the *current monthly expenditure* view with the overspending indicator
against average monthly income. This is the single view the owner would miss
most.

== Increment 2 — Group And Reserve Views

The *group view* (spend per category group) and the reserve requirement for the
call-money account that funds non-monthly fixed costs, derived from fixed-cost
amounts and anchor dates.

== Increment 3 — Cash-Runway, Net Worth And Pension

"How much must be on the current account from today until month end"; net-worth
tracking (life insurance, house value, account balances, investment portfolio,
latest statutory pension figures); and the nominal pension projection (current
pot, retirement age, life expectancy, average inflation, entered nominal return,
and the pension-relevance flag on costs) that yields the pension shortfall.

== Later Increments

Optional Notion/CSV import, richer trend history, backup automation, and — only
if the owner wants app-store presence — a move beyond the PWA. Offline capture
and multi-user sharing remain explicitly out of scope.
