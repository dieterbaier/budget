== Requirements Overview

The owner records transactions from bank statements — each with a booking date,
amount, category, account, and type (expense, income, or transfer). Refunds are
entered as a negative expense in the same category. The application organizes
categories into groups and manages three accounts (current account, a
call-money reserve account for non-monthly fixed costs, and a call-money savings
account) whose balances are entered manually.

Fixed-cost definitions carry an amount, a payment interval (monthly, quarterly,
half-yearly, or yearly), and an anchor date; from a booking the next payment date
and a monthly reserve amount are derived. A monthly income table holds salary and
yields an average income for the overspending comparison.

In scope for the first increments:

* Bootstrap of accounts, category groups, categories, fixed costs, and balances.
* Transaction entry and editing.
* Current monthly expenditure (variable costs plus amortized fixed costs) with an
  overspending signal against average income.
* Spend per category group.
* Reserve requirement for the non-monthly-fixed-cost account.
* Cash runway from today to month end.
* Net-worth tracking (life insurance, house value, account balances, investment
  portfolio, and the latest statutory pension figures).
* Pension projection (nominal), driven by a pension-relevance flag on
  categories, costs, and fixed costs, and yielding the pension shortfall against
  tracked net worth.

Out of scope: bank/API synchronization, cash-payment tracking (beyond a
`Barabhebung` booking), spending forecasts/planning, multi-user or shared
budgets, offline capture, financial advice, and payment execution.
