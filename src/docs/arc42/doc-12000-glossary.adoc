[[glossary]]
= Glossary

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Meaning
| Transaction | A recorded expense, income, or transfer taken from a bank statement.
| Fixed cost | A recurring cost with an amount, an interval (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly), and an anchor date.
| Variable cost | A non-recurring expense recorded as an ordinary transaction.
| Category | A user-defined classification a transaction is assigned to.
| Category group | A grouping of categories (for example dog, car, household, daughter, licensing) used for the group view.
| Account | One of the three tracked accounts: current account, reserve call-money account, savings call-money account.
| Balance snapshot | A manually entered account balance with its "as of" date; not derived from transactions.
| Transfer | Money moved between accounts; never counted as an expense.
| Refund | A negative expense recorded in the same category as the original cost.
| Reserve requirement | Money that must sit on the call-money account to cover non-monthly fixed costs, derived from amounts and anchor dates.
| Overspending signal | Comparison of current monthly expenditure against average monthly income.
| Cash runway | How much must be on the current account from today until month end.
| Pension projection | Nominal estimate of retirement need from pot, retirement age, life expectancy, average inflation, entered nominal return, and pension-relevant costs.
| Pension-relevance flag | A flag on a category, cost, or fixed cost marking whether it still applies in retirement.
| Asset | A manually entered net-worth position (life insurance, house value, account balance, investment portfolio, latest statutory pension figure).
| Net worth | The sum of tracked assets, used against the projected retirement need.
| Pension shortfall | Projected retirement need minus tracked net worth.
| Barabhebung | A cash withdrawal booking; the only cash movement the owner records.
|===
