= Questions And Answers
:id: DOC-005-questions-and-answers

This is the central discussion document for the private Budget project. It
records open and answered questions and the decisions taken. Open questions are
picked up by the generator into the open-questions index for the architecture
decisions chapter.

== Decision Notes

The AI bootstrap seeded this repository from the toolkit's private-budget
example. On 2026-07-24 the owner reviewed the scope and made the first product
and technology decisions in a clarification session. The confirmed product is a
*private, single-user actuals tracker* that replaces the owner's Notion budget:

* Transactions are entered from bank statements. Cash is not tracked, except a
  `Barabhebung` (cash withdrawal) booking. The app analyses spending; it is not
  a full ledger and does not derive account balances.
* Master data: category groups, categories, three accounts (current account, a
  call-money reserve account for non-monthly fixed costs, a call-money savings
  account), fixed-cost definitions (amount, interval, anchor date), and a
  monthly income table.
* Core views: current monthly expenditure with an overspending signal against
  average income; spend per category group; account-reserve requirements; a
  cash runway to month end; net-worth tracking; and a nominal pension projection
  that yields the pension shortfall.
* Clients: an installable React PWA is the only v1 client and covers both
  desktop and mobile. Offline capture and multi-user sharing are out of scope.

The corresponding technology decisions are recorded in ADR-001 and ADR-003
through ADR-008. Content confirmed in that session is marked `reviewed`;
newly derived or still-uncertain content stays `draft`/`proposed`.

== Open Questions For Operations

[[q-ops-003]]
=== Q-OPS-003: Which always-on host runs the backend once multi-device is enabled?

Answer: Open.

The MVP runs locally on a single device. To use the PWA on a phone, the backend
and database must move to an always-on host reachable over HTTPS (a small VPS is
the intended shape). The concrete provider is not yet chosen.

== Answered Questions

[[q-po-001]]
=== Q-PO-001: Should multiple household members share one budget?

Answer: No. The application is single-user with no shared or multi-user data.

[[q-po-002]]
=== Q-PO-002: Is the confirmed product scope a private single-household budgeting app?

Answer: Yes, refined to a private *single-user actuals tracker* that replaces
Notion. Confirmed by the owner on 2026-07-24.

[[q-po-003]]
=== Q-PO-003: Are mobile capture plus backend sufficient for v1, or is a web UI in scope?

Answer: A web UI is in scope and is the primary client. It is delivered as an
installable PWA that also serves mobile use; native mobile apps are out of
scope. See xref:adr-005-web-client-as-pwa[ADR-005].

[[q-arch-001]]
=== Q-ARCH-001: Which technology stack is chosen for backend, mobile client, and datastore?

Answer: Spring Boot (Java 21) hexagonal backend, React + TypeScript + Vite PWA,
and PostgreSQL with JPA and Flyway. See xref:adr-004-backend-stack-and-architecture[ADR-004],
xref:adr-005-web-client-as-pwa[ADR-005], and xref:adr-006-relational-datastore[ADR-006].

[[q-arch-002]]
=== Q-ARCH-002: Are the three proposed ADRs the right initial decisions to pursue?

Answer: ADR-001 (API boundary) and ADR-003 (server-owned calculation) are
confirmed and accepted. ADR-002 (mobile local-first offline entry) is withdrawn
and removed because offline capture is out of scope. New decisions ADR-004
through ADR-008 record the stack, PWA client, datastore, deployment/auth, and
the manual account-balance model.

[[q-ops-001]]
=== Q-OPS-001: Which backup retention period is required?

Answer: The backup approach is decided — a nightly `pg_dump` plus an in-app
export endpoint so the owner is never locked in, with dump copies kept off the
application host. A concrete retention target (keep at least the last twelve
snapshots) is a configuration detail to refine during Increment 1, not an open
architecture question.

[[q-ops-002]]
=== Q-OPS-002: What is the hosting and deployment target for the backend?

Answer: The MVP runs locally (backend plus PostgreSQL) on a single device. When
multi-device use is wanted, backend and database move to a single always-on host
serving all clients over HTTPS. The direction is decided; provider selection is
tracked as Q-OPS-003. See xref:adr-007-single-user-deployment-and-auth[ADR-007].

[[q-po-004]]
=== Q-PO-004: What is the exact pension-projection formula?

Answer: The projection is computed in *nominal* terms. The owner enters an
estimated nominal return (the rate a safe investment would yield today). The
inflation rate is the average of Germany's historical annual inflation rates
from https://www.global-rates.com/de/inflation/historisch/[global-rates.com].
The expected monthly need in retirement is derived from current costs, filtered
by a *pension-relevance* flag carried on categories, individual costs, and fixed
costs (for example, a mortgage repayment is flagged not relevant). The owner also
tracks net worth (life insurance, house value, account balances, investment
portfolio) and the latest statutory pension figures; the projected need minus
tracked net worth yields the *pension shortfall*. The precise compounding
mechanics are refined during the pension increment.
