[[vision-mission]]
= Vision And Mission
:id: DOC-002-vision-mission

Vision: Budget gives its owner a calm, self-hosted view of where the household
money actually goes, without handing personal spending data to a third party.

Mission: Replace the owner's Notion budget with a private, single-user
application that records real bank-statement transactions, keeps them organized
by category and category group, and answers a small set of concrete money
questions:

* How much am I spending this month, and is that more than my average income?
* For which group (house, car, dog, daughter, licensing, ...) do I spend most?
* How much must sit on each account, including the reserve account for
  non-monthly fixed costs?
* Am I on track for retirement given my current pot, costs, inflation, and
  expected return?

Second mission: serve as a working proof of the architecture-knowledge-toolkit.
The project is deliberately run by the toolkit's own method — its skills,
contracts, metamodel and templates — on real work rather than on an example, so
that whether the method holds up, and how it helps day-to-day architectural work,
can be answered from evidence.

The two missions are not in tension, but they are not the same either. The
product mission is finished when the owner stops using Notion. The toolkit
mission is served by *findings*: a place the method helped, a place it got in the
way, a gap worth fixing upstream. Artifact work that produces no finding and
answers no question a later reader would get wrong serves neither mission.
