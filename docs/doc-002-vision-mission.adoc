[[vision-mission]]
= Vision And Mission
:id: DOC-002-vision-mission

Vision: Budget gives its owner a calm, self-hosted view of where the household
money actually goes, without handing personal spending data to a third party.

Mission: Replace the owner's Notion budget with a private, single-user
application that records real bank-statement transactions, keeps them organized
by category and category group, and answers a small set of concrete money
questions:

* How much am I spending this month, and is that more than my average income?
* For which group (house, car, dog, daughter, licensing, ...) do I spend most?
* How much must sit on each account, including the reserve account for
  non-monthly fixed costs?
* Am I on track for retirement given my current pot, costs, inflation, and
  expected return?
