== Stakeholders

The owner is the sole user and also the developer and operator. The roles below
are the same person wearing different hats; they are kept distinct to clarify
concerns.

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Stakeholder | Concern
| Owner / user | Fast entry from bank statements, trustworthy monthly and group views, a sound pension projection, and privacy.
| Developer | Clean hexagonal boundaries, TDD and Gherkin, swappable datastore and delivery.
| Operator | Recoverable data (backups), simple local-then-hosted deployment, observable failures.
| Toolkit author | Whether the architecture-knowledge-toolkit's method holds up on real work: which skills earn their keep, where the metamodel or the generators fall short, and what should change upstream. This hat is served by findings rather than by artifact count.
|===
