[[quality-goals]]
== Quality Goals

[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Goal | Rationale

| QG-001
| Privacy
| Personal spending data is self-hosted and must stay protected from unauthorized access.

| QG-002
| Data integrity
| Totals and derived figures must reflect the recorded transactions without silent loss or duplication.

| QG-003
| Calculation correctness
| Monthly expenditure, reserve requirements, and the pension projection must be correct and covered by tests.

| QG-004
| Usability
| Recording a transaction from a bank statement must be fast enough to become a habit.

| QG-005
| Resource efficiency
| The application should not spend bytes, requests, or machine time it does not need. Shipping less and computing less is a design choice, not a side effect, and it is weighed openly when decisions are made.
|===

QG-005 is deliberately last, and it is a *tiebreaker rather than an override*: it
must never outrank QG-001 to QG-004. A smaller bundle is not worth a correctness
gap, and no resource argument justifies weakening privacy or dropping tests. See
xref:adr-019-resource-efficiency[ADR-019] for how it is weighed, what is enforced,
and the honest limit of what it currently measures.
