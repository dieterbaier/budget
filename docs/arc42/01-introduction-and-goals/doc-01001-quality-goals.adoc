[[quality-goals]]
== Quality Goals

[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Goal | Rationale

| QG-001
| Privacy
| Personal spending data is self-hosted and must stay protected from unauthorized access.

| QG-002
| Data integrity
| Totals and derived figures must reflect the recorded transactions without silent loss or duplication.

| QG-003
| Calculation correctness
| Monthly expenditure, reserve requirements, and the pension projection must be correct and covered by tests.

| QG-004
| Usability
| Recording a transaction from a bank statement must be fast enough to become a habit.
|===
