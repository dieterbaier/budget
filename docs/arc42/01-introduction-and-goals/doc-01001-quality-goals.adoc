== Quality Goals

[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Goal | Rationale

| QG-001
| Privacy
| Personal spending data is self-hosted and must stay protected from unauthorized access.

| QG-002
| Data integrity
| Totals and derived figures must reflect the recorded transactions without silent loss or duplication.

| QG-003
| Calculation correctness
| Monthly expenditure, reserve requirements, and the pension projection must be correct and covered by tests.

| QG-004
| Usability
| Recording a transaction from a bank statement must be fast enough to become a habit.

| QG-005
| Resource frugality
| The application avoids unnecessary transfer, computation, storage, requests, and infrastructure capacity. When otherwise suitable options are comparable, the option requiring fewer resources is preferred.
|===

QG-005 is deliberately named *frugality* rather than sustainability or GreenIT.
The project can state that shipping 13.7 kB less JavaScript means less to
transfer, store, decompress and parse. It cannot yet state how many watt-hours or
grams of CO₂ that saves, or whether the saving is material beside hosting and
end-user-device costs. The measured fact and the environmental hypothesis are
kept distinct, so the goal does not claim an impact nobody here has measured.

=== Resolving a conflict between goals

Quality goals are not interchangeable points in a weighted score. A reviewer
facing a genuine conflict applies this rule, in order:

. *Required quality scenarios are non-compensable minimums.* An option that
  violates a required scenario for correctness, privacy, security, or fitness for
  purpose is not acceptable, whatever it saves.
. *Compare only the acceptable options.* For each, state which goals improve,
  which regress, by how much, and at what evidence level.
. *Prefer a Pareto improvement.* An option no worse on the required goals and
  cheaper in resources wins outright.
. *Resolve a real trade-off against the concrete scenario*, not an abstract
  score: whose outcome changes, under what conditions, and whether that
  regression is accepted.
. *Apply QG-005 when the options are otherwise comparable.* The frugal option
  wins the tie.
. *Record the assumptions and a revisit trigger.* If the decision rests on a
  hypothesis, state what measurement or structural change would reopen it.

The rule exists to prevent one specific failure: a weighted score in which enough
bundle-size points mathematically pay for a privacy or correctness defect. Saving
resources never buys a regression in something the product owes its user.

See xref:adr-019-resource-efficiency[ADR-019] for the evidence levels a claim
must declare, what is enforced today, and the honest limit of what it measures.
