== QS-001: Budget Total Integrity

=== Scenario

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Element | Description
| Source | Owner.
| Stimulus | Adds, edits, or deletes a transaction (including a refund or a transfer).
| Artifact | Monthly expenditure, group totals, and reserve figures.
| Environment | Normal operation.
| Response | The figures reflect the committed change exactly once; transfers are excluded and refunds net against their category.
| Response Measure | Domain and application tests cover create, edit, delete, refund, and transfer cases with no unexplained drift.
|===
