= Glossary

This is the term register. Per ADR-022 every domain concept has exactly one entry
here, and the entry fixes both forms the concept may take: the *term* the owner
speaks, and the *identifier* used in `domain/model/` and in the architecture
documentation. Where the two differ, the entry is the agreement that they name
one concept.

The *display* column carries the form the interface uses, per ADR-023. It is
presentation only and appears in no model, payload or document. A dash means the
identifier is already the word on screen.

Umlauts are transliterated in identifiers — `Vermoegen`, not `Vermögen` — so one
spelling serves Java, SQL, JSON and TypeScript. The display form carries the real
spelling, which is the only place it is read by a human.

Three concepts came from the architecture rather than from the domain and were
*adopted* under ADR-022 rule 4 rather than translated: `Auswertungszeitraum`,
`Restmonatsbedarf` and `Ausgabenwarnung`.

Wherever prose names a domain concept it uses the identifier, in backticks — a
`Fixkostenposition`, not a fixed cost. ADR-022 rule 3 already requires the
identifier in architecture documentation; the backticks are what stop a
mixed-language sentence from reading as an accident.

[cols="1,1,1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Identifier | Display | Meaning

| Transaktion
| `Transaction`
| Transaktion
| A recorded expense, income or transfer taken from a bank statement, with a name (the payee or what it was). A payment of a `Fixkostenposition` is *not* recorded as a `Transaction`.

| Fixkosten
| `Fixkostenposition`
| Fixkosten
| A recurring cost, defined once and never booked: a payee, an amount per cycle, a `Zahlungsintervall`, a `Category`, an optional `Verwendungszweck`, a `Zahlungsmethode`, and a `letzteZahlung`. The spoken term is plural and names the whole class; the identifier is the singular position, because the model holds one at a time.

| variable Kosten
| `VariableKosten`
| Variable Kosten
| Any recorded expense that is not a `Fixkostenposition`. Averaged over the `Auswertungszeitraum` to give its monthly figure. A classification of `Transaction` rather than something stored, so the identifier appears in calculations and not in the schema.

| Einkünfte
| `Einkuenfte`
| Einkünfte
| What the owner earns, recorded per month. The `Ausgabenwarnung` compares the long-run monthly cost against the average of these.

| Zahlungsintervall
| `Zahlungsintervall`
| —
| The cycle on which a `Fixkostenposition` falls due: monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly or two-yearly. Dividing the amount per cycle by the months in it gives the `MonthlyPortion`.

| Kategorie
| `Category`
| Kategorie
| A user-defined classification a `Transaction` is assigned to; holds `Verwendungszweck` entries and belongs to one `CategoryGroup`.

| Kategoriegruppe
| `CategoryGroup`
| Kategoriegruppe
| A grouping of `Category` entries (for example dog, car, household, daughter, licensing) used for the group view.

| Verwendungszweck
| `Verwendungszweck`
| —
| The banking term for what a payment is for ("Fernwärme", "Streaming (Musik)"), one level below the `Category`. Belongs to exactly one `Category`; optional on a `Transaction` and on a `Fixkostenposition`. Kept German because `Purpose` is generic where the original is specific.

| Auswertungszeitraum
| `Auswertungszeitraum`
| —
| The period the averages are computed over: from the first recorded booking to today, measured day-precise rather than in whole months. Adopted, not translated.

| letzte Zahlung
| `letzteZahlung`
| Letzte Zahlung
| The date a `Fixkostenposition` was last paid. Maintained by hand, moves forward, and drives both `naechsteZahlung` and `Sollansparung`. Kept German because the English rendering `anchorDate` invented a fixed reference point the domain does not have.

| nächste Zahlung
| `naechsteZahlung`
| Nächste Zahlung
| `letzteZahlung` plus one `Zahlungsintervall`. No business-day correction — a due date on a Sunday is left as it is, because nothing is executed from it.

| fällige Fixkosten
| `faelligeFixkostenposition`
| Fällige Fixkosten
| A `Fixkostenposition` whose `naechsteZahlung` has arrived. Used to catch a payment missed while transcribing, and to spot a `Fixkostenposition` that no longer exists (its `letzteZahlung` is older than its own interval).

| Zahlungsmethode
| `Zahlungsmethode`
| —
| Direct debit, credit card or PayPal. A value object inside a `Fixkostenposition`, recorded for information only; enters no calculation.

| Konto
| `Account`
| Konto
| One of the three tracked accounts: current account, reserve call-money account, savings call-money account.

| Kontostand
| `BalanceSnapshot`
| Kontostand
| A manually entered `Account` balance with its "as of" date; not derived from `Transaction` records. The identifier keeps "snapshot" because the German word names the value and drops the point in time, which is the half that matters here.

| Umbuchung
| `Umbuchung`
| —
| Money moved between the owner's own `Account` records; never counted as an expense. Kept German because "Überweisung" would also cover payments to third parties, which are expenses.

| Erstattung
| `Erstattung`
| —
| A booking with a negative amount in the same `Category` as the original cost. A group total may legitimately be negative. Also spoken as "Gutschrift"; the two are one concept here, and `Erstattung` is the registered term.

| Erstattungen pro Monat
| `ErstattungenProMonat`
| Erstattungen pro Monat
| The sum of the negative amounts in a `CategoryGroup`, shown beside the net figure so gross and net are both readable.

| Sollansparung
| `Sollansparung`
| —
| What should have been saved up by now on the reserve call-money `Account` to cover a non-monthly `Fixkostenposition`: whole months elapsed since its `letzteZahlung`, times its `MonthlyPortion`. Zero when the `Zahlungsintervall` is monthly. Kept German because "reserve requirement" names what must be held, which is a different quantity that happens to be equal today.

| Monatsanteil
| `MonthlyPortion`
| Monatsanteil
| A `Fixkostenposition`'s amount per cycle divided by the months in its `Zahlungsintervall`, which is how it enters the monthly cost figure.

| Ausgabenwarnung
| `Ausgabenwarnung`
| —
| Comparison of the long-run monthly cost against average monthly `Einkuenfte`, raised when cost exceeds income. Savings and pension contributions count as costs, deliberately. Adopted, not translated.

| Restmonatsbedarf
| `Restmonatsbedarf`
| —
| What must be on the current `Account` from today to month end: the remaining days times the daily rate of `VariableKosten`, plus every `faelligeFixkostenposition` still due this month. An amount, not a duration — which is why it is not called a runway. Adopted, not translated.

| Rentenprognose
| `Rentenprognose`
| —
| Nominal estimate of the monthly need in retirement, computed twice — with inflation only, and with inflation plus an estimated `CostIncrease`. Kept German with the rest of the `Renten-` concepts, because "pension" and "Rente" are not the same institution.

| Inflationsraten
| `InflationSeries`
| Inflationsraten
| The historical annual inflation rates, entered by the owner. The application averages them itself rather than taking a single typed-in rate.

| Kostensteigerung
| `CostIncrease`
| Kostensteigerung
| A single estimated percentage on top of inflation, giving the second `Rentenprognose`.

| rentenrelevant
| `rentenrelevant`
| Rentenrelevant
| Marks whether a cost still applies in retirement. The `Category` carries the default; a `Transaction` or a `Fixkostenposition` may override it, and the override wins.

| Vermögensposition
| `Vermoegensposition`
| Vermögensposition
| One manually entered entry in the `Vermoegen`: life insurance, house value, investment portfolio, the latest statutory pension figure, or the cash across the `Account` records, which the owner calls "Geldguthaben". Carries a minimum, likely and maximum value.

| Vermögen
| `Vermoegen`
| Vermögen
| The sum of the tracked `Vermoegensposition` entries, as a minimum/likely/maximum band, used against the projected retirement need.

| Rentenlücke
| `Rentenluecke`
| Rentenlücke
| `Rentenprognose` minus `Vermoegen`.

| Barabhebung
| `Barabhebung`
| —
| A cash withdrawal booking; the only cash movement the owner records.
|===
