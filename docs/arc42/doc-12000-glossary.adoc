[[glossary]]
= Glossary

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Meaning
| Transaction | A recorded expense, income, or transfer taken from a bank statement, with a name (the payee or what it was). Fixed-cost payments are *not* transactions — see Fixed cost.
| Fixed cost | A recurring cost, defined once and never booked: a payee, an amount per cycle, an interval, a category, an optional purpose, a payment method, and the date of the last payment.
| Variable cost | Any recorded expense that is not a fixed cost. Averaged over the tracking window to give its monthly figure.
| Category | A user-defined classification a transaction is assigned to; holds purposes and belongs to one category group.
| Category group | A grouping of categories (for example dog, car, household, daughter, licensing) used for the group view.
| Purpose | German `Verwendungszweck`. What the money was actually for (`Fernwärme`, `Streaming (Musik)`), one level below the category. Belongs to exactly one category; optional on a transaction and on a fixed cost.
| Tracking window | The period the averages are computed over: from the first recorded booking to today, measured day-precise rather than in whole months.
| Last payment | The date a fixed cost was last paid. Maintained by hand, moves forward, and drives both the next payment date and the reserve requirement.
| Next payment | Last payment plus one interval. No business-day correction — a due date on a Sunday is left as it is, because nothing is executed from it.
| Due fixed cost | A fixed cost whose next payment has arrived. Used to catch a payment missed while transcribing, and to spot a fixed cost that no longer exists (its last payment is older than its own interval).
| Payment method | Direct debit, credit card or PayPal. Recorded for information only; enters no calculation.
| Account | One of the three tracked accounts: current account, reserve call-money account, savings call-money account.
| Balance snapshot | A manually entered account balance with its "as of" date; not derived from transactions.
| Transfer | Money moved between accounts; never counted as an expense.
| Refund | A booking with a negative amount in the same category as the original cost. A group total may legitimately be negative.
| Refunds per month | The sum of the negative amounts in a group, shown beside the net figure so gross and net are both readable.
| Reserve requirement | Money that must sit on the call-money reserve account to cover non-monthly fixed costs: whole months elapsed since the last payment, times the monthly portion. Zero for monthly fixed costs.
| Monthly portion | A fixed cost's amount per cycle divided by the months in its cycle, which is how it enters the monthly cost figure.
| Overspending signal | Comparison of the long-run monthly cost against average monthly income. Savings and pension contributions count as costs, deliberately.
| Cash runway | What must be on the current account from today to month end: the remaining days times the daily rate of the variable costs, plus the fixed costs still due this month.
| Pension projection | Nominal estimate of the monthly need in retirement, computed twice — with inflation only, and with inflation plus an estimated cost increase.
| Inflation series | The historical annual inflation rates, entered by the owner. The application averages them itself rather than taking a single typed-in rate.
| Cost increase | A single estimated percentage on top of inflation, giving the second pension projection.
| Pension-relevance flag | Marks whether a cost still applies in retirement. The category carries the default; a transaction or fixed cost may override it, and the override wins.
| Asset | A manually entered net-worth position (life insurance, house value, account balance, investment portfolio, latest statutory pension figure), carrying a minimum, likely and maximum value.
| Net worth | The sum of tracked assets, as a minimum/likely/maximum band, used against the projected retirement need.
| Pension shortfall | Projected retirement need minus tracked net worth.
| Barabhebung | A cash withdrawal booking; the only cash movement the owner records.
|===
