= Glossary

This is the term register. Per ADR-022 every domain concept has exactly one entry
here, and the entry fixes both forms the concept may take: the *term* the owner
speaks, and the *identifier* used in `domain/model/` and in the architecture
documentation. Where the two differ, the entry is the agreement that they name
one concept.

The *display* column carries the form the interface uses, per ADR-023. It is
presentation only and appears in no model, payload or document. A dash means the
identifier is already the word on screen.

Umlauts are transliterated in identifiers — `Vermoegen`, not `Vermögen` — so one
spelling serves Java, SQL, JSON and TypeScript. The display form carries the real
spelling, which is the only place it is read by a human.

Three concepts came from the architecture rather than from the domain and were
*adopted* under ADR-022 rule 4 rather than translated: `Auswertungszeitraum`,
`Restmonatsbedarf` and `Ausgabenwarnung`.

[cols="1,1,1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Identifier | Display | Meaning

| Transaktion
| `Transaction`
| Transaktion
| A recorded expense, income or transfer taken from a bank statement, with a name (the payee or what it was). Fixed-cost payments are *not* Transaktionen — see Fixkosten.

| Fixkosten
| `Fixkostenposition`
| Fixkosten
| A recurring cost, defined once and never booked: a payee, an amount per cycle, an interval, a category, an optional Verwendungszweck, a Zahlungsart, and the letzte Zahlung. The spoken term is plural and names the whole class; the identifier is the singular position, because the model holds one at a time.

| variable Kosten
| `VariableKosten`
| Variable Kosten
| Any recorded expense that is not Fixkosten. Averaged over the Auswertungszeitraum to give its monthly figure. A classification of Transaktionen rather than something stored, so the identifier appears in calculations and not in the schema.

| Kategorie
| `Category`
| Kategorie
| A user-defined classification a Transaktion is assigned to; holds Verwendungszwecke and belongs to one Kategoriegruppe.

| Kategoriegruppe
| `CategoryGroup`
| Kategoriegruppe
| A grouping of Kategorien (for example dog, car, household, daughter, licensing) used for the group view.

| Verwendungszweck
| `Verwendungszweck`
| —
| The banking term for what a payment is for (`Fernwärme`, `Streaming (Musik)`), one level below the Kategorie. Belongs to exactly one Kategorie; optional on a Transaktion and on Fixkosten. Kept German because `Purpose` is generic where the original is specific.

| Auswertungszeitraum
| `Auswertungszeitraum`
| —
| The period the averages are computed over: from the first recorded booking to today, measured day-precise rather than in whole months. Adopted, not translated.

| letzte Zahlung
| `letzteZahlung`
| Letzte Zahlung
| The date Fixkosten were last paid. Maintained by hand, moves forward, and drives both the nächste Zahlung and the Sollansparung. Kept German because the English rendering `anchorDate` invented a fixed reference point the domain does not have.

| nächste Zahlung
| `naechsteZahlung`
| Nächste Zahlung
| Letzte Zahlung plus one interval. No business-day correction — a due date on a Sunday is left as it is, because nothing is executed from it.

| fällige Fixkosten
| `faelligeFixkostenposition`
| Fällige Fixkosten
| Fixkosten whose nächste Zahlung has arrived. Used to catch a payment missed while transcribing, and to spot Fixkosten that no longer exist (their letzte Zahlung is older than their own interval).

| Zahlungsart
| `PaymentMethod`
| Zahlungsart
| Direct debit, credit card or PayPal. Recorded for information only; enters no calculation.

| Konto
| `Account`
| Konto
| One of the three tracked accounts: current account, reserve call-money account, savings call-money account.

| Kontostand
| `BalanceSnapshot`
| Kontostand
| A manually entered account balance with its "as of" date; not derived from Transaktionen. The identifier keeps "snapshot" because the German word names the value and drops the point in time, which is the half that matters here.

| Umbuchung
| `Umbuchung`
| —
| Money moved between the owner's own Konten; never counted as an expense. Kept German because `Überweisung` would also cover payments to third parties, which are expenses.

| Erstattung
| `Erstattung`
| —
| A booking with a negative amount in the same Kategorie as the original cost. A group total may legitimately be negative. Also spoken as "Gutschrift"; the two are one concept here, and `Erstattung` is the registered term.

| Erstattungen pro Monat
| `ErstattungenProMonat`
| Erstattungen pro Monat
| The sum of the negative amounts in a Kategoriegruppe, shown beside the net figure so gross and net are both readable.

| Sollansparung
| `Sollansparung`
| —
| What should have been saved up by now on the reserve call-money account to cover non-monthly Fixkosten: whole months elapsed since the letzte Zahlung, times the Monatsanteil. Zero for monthly Fixkosten. Kept German because `reserve requirement` names what must be held, which is a different quantity that happens to be equal today.

| Monatsanteil
| `MonthlyPortion`
| Monatsanteil
| A Fixkostenposition's amount per cycle divided by the months in its cycle, which is how it enters the monthly cost figure.

| Ausgabenwarnung
| `Ausgabenwarnung`
| —
| Comparison of the long-run monthly cost against average monthly income, raised when cost exceeds income. Savings and pension contributions count as costs, deliberately. Adopted, not translated.

| Restmonatsbedarf
| `Restmonatsbedarf`
| —
| What must be on the current account from today to month end: the remaining days times the daily rate of the variable Kosten, plus the Fixkosten still due this month. An amount, not a duration — which is why it is not called a runway. Adopted, not translated.

| Rentenprognose
| `Rentenprognose`
| —
| Nominal estimate of the monthly need in retirement, computed twice — with inflation only, and with inflation plus an estimated Kostensteigerung. Kept German with the rest of the Renten- concepts, because "pension" and "Rente" are not the same institution.

| Inflationsraten
| `InflationSeries`
| Inflationsraten
| The historical annual inflation rates, entered by the owner. The application averages them itself rather than taking a single typed-in rate.

| Kostensteigerung
| `CostIncrease`
| Kostensteigerung
| A single estimated percentage on top of inflation, giving the second Rentenprognose.

| rentenrelevant
| `rentenrelevant`
| Rentenrelevant
| Marks whether a cost still applies in retirement. The Kategorie carries the default; a Transaktion or Fixkosten may override it, and the override wins.

| Vermögensposition
| `Vermoegensposition`
| Vermögensposition
| One manually entered entry in the Vermögen: life insurance, house value, investment portfolio, the latest statutory pension figure, or the Geldguthaben across the Konten. Carries a minimum, likely and maximum value.

| Vermögen
| `Vermoegen`
| Vermögen
| The sum of the tracked Vermögenspositionen, as a minimum/likely/maximum band, used against the projected retirement need.

| Rentenlücke
| `Rentenluecke`
| Rentenlücke
| Rentenprognose minus Vermögen.

| Barabhebung
| `Barabhebung`
| —
| A cash withdrawal booking; the only cash movement the owner records.
|===
