== ADR-009: Domain Identity And DDD Tactical Design

=== Status

Accepted. Confirmed by the owner on 2026-07-24.

=== Decision

The domain uses DDD tactical building blocks:

* *Value objects* have no identity and compare by value: `Money`,
  `PaymentInterval`, and the typed identifiers themselves.
* *Entities* have identity expressed as a *domain* concept — a natural business
  key where a stable one exists (`Category` by name, `Account` by IBAN/name,
  `IncomeEntry` by month) or a domain-generated typed identifier (for example
  `TransactionId` as a UUID) where none does.
* *Aggregates* group entities under a root; the root is the unit of consistency
  and the granularity of repositories.

Persistence surrogate keys (JPA `@Id` columns), if any, stay inside the JPA
entities and never appear in the domain model.

=== Context

The owner explicitly prefers business identity over technical identity. DDD's
actual rule is not "no ids" but "entities have identity, value objects do not";
the anti-pattern is letting a database autoincrement become the domain's notion
of identity. A `Transaction` needs identity (two identical-looking expenses on
the same day are distinct), but bank statements do not always carry a stable
reference — hence the natural-or-generated identity rule and Q-ARCH-003.

Initial aggregate map (refined as slices land):

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Aggregate root | Identity | Notes
| Account | IBAN or name | Owns manually entered balance snapshots (value objects).
| CategoryGroup | Name | Groups categories.
| Category | Name | Reference entity; carries the pension-relevance flag.
| Transaction | Bank reference or generated `TransactionId` | See Q-ARCH-003.
| FixedCost | Generated id | References a category; has amount, interval, anchor date.
| IncomeEntry | Month | One recorded monthly income per month.
|===

=== Considered Options

==== Domain identity: natural keys plus typed ids (chosen)

Entities carry business identity or a domain-generated typed id; the domain is
free of persistence concerns.

==== Leak the JPA surrogate `Long id` into the domain

Simplest to persist, but ties identity to the database and violates the owner's
preference and DDD.

==== Treat transactions as identity-less value objects

Impossible to edit or delete an individual transaction; loses required identity.

=== Consequences

==== Positive

* The domain is expressed in business terms; identity is meaningful and testable.
* Aggregates give clear repository boundaries.

==== Negative

* A typed-id/mapping layer is needed between domain identity and JPA rows.

==== Neutral or Follow-Up

* Introduce `TransactionId` with the edit/delete slice; decide its source in
  Q-ARCH-003. Refine aggregate boundaries as accounts, groups, and pension land.
